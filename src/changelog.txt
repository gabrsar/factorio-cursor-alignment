Version: 0.3.0
Date: 2026-09-30
  Features:
    - Added a per-player configuration panel, opened with Ctrl+Shift+O or the top button.

    - Hover activation now snaps the key event's cursor position to a tile, including negative coordinates.

    - Held Shift and continuous grid snapping for selection/copy tools remain unavailable through the public API.
